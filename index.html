About Me

I'm a 3D Artist based in Cairo, Egypt, specializing in furniture and product visualization using Blender. My work focuses on photorealistic rendering, realistic materials, lighting, scene composition, and interior staging.

I have worked as a freelance 3D Artist with MaxHomz, creating furniture visualizations for commercial presentation. I also developed iVision Camera, a real-time traffic-monitoring simulation project in Unreal Engine 5.
